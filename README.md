# Sleeve Studio

A browser design tool for printed arm sleeves. One HTML file, nothing to install.

- 8 ready-made looks and 12 stackable presets
- Pen painting with pressure: ink, marker, dry brush, spray
- Flat template, flat-lay pair and turnable on-arm views
- Print check for thin lines, the elbow zone and seams
- Exports: print SVG, 300 dpi PNG, flat-lay PNG, on-arm PNG

Your work saves in your own browser. The template is 46 cm long, 17 cm at the top and 11 cm at the wrist; use your printer's template for the final run.
